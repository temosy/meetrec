# Privacy Policy

MeetRec does not collect analytics, does not use third-party tracking, and does not upload recordings.

Recordings are created locally on your Mac and saved to the folder you choose. Screen, system audio, and microphone data are used only for recording on the device.

Microphone recording is off by default. MeetRec requests microphone permission only when you enable microphone recording.

For questions, open an issue at <https://github.com/temosy/meetrec/issues>.
